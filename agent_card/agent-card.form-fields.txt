Tags: financial-services, investigation-brief, multi-agent, sql-and-rag, markdown, human-review
Example: For the latest observed month, investigate the highest-exposure flagged account. Combine the exact portfolio and account SQL totals, five transaction-evidence patterns, and the applicable retrieved triage policy. Return a USD Markdown analyst brief with separate portfolio/account tables, policy citations, evidence gaps and human-review steps.
